5 FD Examples with Calculation:
₹10,000 FD for 1 year at 6.8%: Interest = ₹10,000 × 6.8% = ₹680. Maturity = ₹10,680.
₹50,000 FD for 2 years at 6.8% (quarterly compounding):
A = 50,000 × (1 + 0.068/4)^(4×2) = 50,000 × 1.1437 = ₹57,185. Interest = ₹7,185.
₹1,00,000 FD for 5 years at 6.5% (annual compounding):
A = 1,00,000 × (1.065)^5 = 1,00,000 × 1.370 = ₹1,37,000. Interest = ₹37,000.
₹5,000/month FD Laddering Strategy:
A 1-year FD of ₹5,000 every month. 12 FDs in 12 months. One FD matures every month = ₹5,000 + interest liquid. Best for regular income.
Tax-Saving FD ₹1,50,000 (5 years):
Principal = ₹1,50,000. Interest 6.5% = ₹9,750/year. 5 years = ₹48,750 interest. Maturity = ₹1,98,750. Tax benefit 80C = ₹46,800 saved (at 30% slab). Effective return = much higher.

Premature Withdrawal:
Penalty 0.5-1% interest rate cut. Example: 1% penalty on 6.5% FD = 5.5% actual. If a 5-year FD is broken in 6 months, there will be a loss. Should only be broken in an emergency.

Tax on FD Interest:
Section 80TTA: Savings account interest up to ₹10,000 is tax-free. Does not apply to FD interest.
TDS: 10% TDS is deducted on FD interest if interest exceeds ₹40,000+/year (₹50,000+ for seniors).
Form 15G: If total income is not taxable, submit Form 15G — TDS will not be deducted.

6.3 Recurring Deposit (RD) — Monthly Saving Habit
In an RD, you deposit a fixed amount every month, and get a lump sum at maturity. Best for students because you can deposit a small amount monthly. Can be started from ₹100!

How RD Works:
Step 1: Decide amount (₹500/month) and tenure (5 years).
Step 2: Auto-debit setup — fixed amount cut on a fixed date every month.
Step 3: Get lump sum + interest at maturity.

6.4 Digital Payments — UPI, NEFT, IMPS, RTGS
UPI (Unified Payments Interface):
What it is: NPCI's system that links bank accounts for instant transfers. Why it's free: Promoted by government for Digital India. Daily limit: Usually ₹1 lakh/day (some banks ₹2 lakh). Best for: Daily payments, P2P transfer, merchant payment.

NEFT (National Electronic Funds Transfer):
Speed: Up to 30 min (half-hourly batch settlement). Limit: No limit. Charges: Free for online (since Jan 2020). Availability: 24/7. Best for: Large transfers, non-urgent.

IMPS (Immediate Payment Service):
Speed: Instant. Limit: ₹5 lakh. Charges: ₹5-15. Availability: 24/7. Best for: Urgent transfer, any time.

RTGS (Real Time Gross Settlement):
Speed: Instant. Limit: ₹2 lakh minimum. Charges: Free online (since Jan 2020); ₹25-50 at branch. Availability: 24/7 (now). Best for: Very large transfers.

Which one is best for a student?
Debit card: For daily use. Your own money, no debt risk. Must have.
Credit card: Only if disciplined. Secured card against FD is best. Build CIBIL.
Prepaid card: Like a gift card. Load money, use it. Good for budget control.
ATM card: Outdated. Debit card includes ATM feature.

6.7 KYC and Online Safety — 15 Safety Tips
KYC = Know Your Customer:
This is a verification process in which the bank verifies your identity. Documents: Aadhaar + PAN + Photo. Done online in 2 minutes via Aadhaar e-KYC. Video KYC is also available in some banks — account gets opened sitting at home.

15 Safety Tips for Online Banking:
1. Do not share OTP with anyone — even bank employees won't ask. OTP = password. Shared = account emptied.
2. Do not click on phishing links — fake bank links in email/SMS. Check URL — sbi.co.in vs sbi-bank.co.in (fake).
3. Do not do banking on public WiFi — data can be stolen. Man-in-the-middle attack possible. Use mobile data.
4. Keep password strong — not date of birth. Mix of uppercase, lowercase, numbers, symbols. 12+ characters.
5. Turn on 2-factor authentication — SMS OTP + app notification. Double security.
6. Set screen lock on phone — PIN, pattern, fingerprint. If phone is stolen, banking app remains safe.
7. Logout of banking app after use — Auto-lock 2 minutes. Don't stay logged in.
8. Do not give bank details on call from unknown numbers — Bank never asks for OTP/CVV over call. Caller = scammer.
9. Change UPI PIN regularly — Once in 3 months. Don't keep 1234, 0000, or birth date.
10. Protect yourself from SIM swap fraud — remain alert for duplicate SIM. If network suddenly vanishes = call customer care immediately.
11. Avoid loan apps — 90% are fraud. Chinese loan apps, harassment, fake interest. Take loans only from government-registered banks.
12. Ignore "Lottery/prize" SMS — 100% scam. "You won ₹5 lakh" = trap. Nobody gives ₹5 lakh for free.
13. Do not scan QR code from unknown sources — Scanning QR = paying, not receiving. "Scan to receive money" = scam.
14. Do not invest money based on Instagram/WhatsApp investment tips — "Guaranteed 20% return" = 100% scam. Consult only SEBI registered advisors.
15. Use only bank's official number/customer care — Use number printed on card or from official website. Do not search "SBI customer care" on Google — fake numbers appear.

WHERE PEOPLE MAKE MISTAKES (Module 6)
- Not opening a zero-balance account — Minimum balance penalty deducted monthly. ₹100-₹600/quarter = ₹400-₹2,400/year wasted.
- Banking on public WiFi — High risk of data theft. Cafes, stations, malls — never do banking there.
- Sharing OTP — To no one, bank staff doesn't ask either. OTP = key to account.
- Premature withdrawal of FD repeatedly — Penalty + lower return. Keep emergency fund separate so FD doesn't need to be broken.
- Keeping multiple UPI apps — Increases both confusion and security risk. One app, one PIN, simple.
- Saving debit card details on unknown websites — Don't click "Remember card". Will leak in data breach.
- Not checking e-statements — Hidden charges won't be noticed. Check monthly for 5 minutes.
- Leaving bank account dormant — Inactivity charges + account freeze. Make one transaction every 3 months.

KEY TAKEAWAYS (Module 6)
- Student zero-balance savings account is best — no minimum balance tension. SBI, Kotak, HDFC all offer it.
- FD and RD are safe options — guaranteed return with zero market risk. RD starts from ₹100. FD from ₹1,000.
- UPI is best for daily payments — instant, free, 24/7. Limit ₹1 lakh/day.
- Check e-statements monthly to avoid hidden charges — ₹2,000-₹5,000/year savings possible.
- Never share OTP with anyone — this is the golden rule of banking safety. 90% of frauds start with OTP.

COMMON MISCONCEPTIONS (Module 6)
- "Everything at bank is safe" — Wrong! Online frauds are common, awareness is necessary. Bank account is safe, but your phone can be unsafe.
- "No returns higher than FD anywhere" — Wrong! SIP has historically given 12-15% return in the long term. FD 6.5% = barely beats inflation.
- "Debit card and credit card are the same" — No! Debit = your money, Credit = debt. Credit card = bank's money, 36-48% interest.
- "Online banking is risky" — Safe if you follow rules — don't share OTP, avoid phishing, keep strong password.
- "KYC is a one-time formality" — No! Periodic KYC update is necessary to keep account active. Update every 2-3 years.
